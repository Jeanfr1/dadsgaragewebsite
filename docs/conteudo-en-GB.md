## Service descriptions

**General maintenance:** Maintenance and repair for motorcycles of any make, model and cc. Talk to the team about your bike and the work it needs.

**Improving performance:** Discuss performance work with the workshop. The team will talk through your motorcycle, your riding and the changes you have in mind.

**Restore:** Bring your restoration project to the team and discuss what is needed to get your motorcycle back where it belongs.

**Customising:** Customising and reinvention for a motorcycle that feels like your own. Tell the team what you have in mind.

**Claims & recovery:** Dad’s Garage offers accident claims support and motorcycle recovery. Contact the team to discuss the situation and the next steps.
