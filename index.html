Claims & recovery

Make your
claim.

Had an accident? Dad’s Garage can help you claim your losses.

Call +44 7472 919010
  1. 01

    Post-accident safety

    After your accident, make sure no one is hurt. If anyone is, call an ambulance and the police.

  2. 02

    Information exchange

    Exchange details with the third party:

    • Name, address and phone number
    • Insurance company
    • Vehicle colour, make, model and plate number
    • Details of a witness, if possible
    • Photos and videos of the scene and the vehicles
    • The exact location of the accident
  3. 03

    Rapid response

    Once everyone is safe, call the team on +44 7472 919010 and tell us what happened.

  4. 04

    Vehicle rescue and loss recovery

    We can rescue your vehicle. We work with a specialist partner to claim your losses and get you a hire vehicle as quickly as possible.

The team

More thana garage.

A lifestyle of people who are passionate about two wheels. To open this garage was a family dream for a long time, and it came alive when Paulo and Ricardo came together to make it happen.

  • Paulo

    Chief mechanic

    The heart of the workshop. Rider for life and mechanic for love, with a start in sales at a multi-brand motorcycle shop and more than ten years as a chief mechanic in London workshops. Professional, honest and a perfectionist.

  • Ricardo

    Manager · Head of accident claims

    Runs the garage, its accident claims, the finances and customer service. He spent six years at one of the country’s leading claims companies and has a degree in business.

  • Aline

    Finance & administration

    Looks after the financial and administrative side. Her experience in business management brings efficiency and organisation, and keeps the workshop running smoothly.

  • Lisa

    Creative & social media

    The creative soul of the garage, behind the visual communication and social media, from every colour on our pages to the marketing materials and the workshop’s interior design.
